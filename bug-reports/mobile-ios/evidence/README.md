# Evidence Handling

Only redacted, publication-ready evidence belongs in this directory.

Before committing a screenshot, recording, or log, remove or obscure:

- names, faces, email addresses, and phone numbers;
- account, order, tracking, and device identifiers;
- addresses, precise locations, QR codes, and barcodes;
- payment data, authentication data, and private conversations;
- employer or internal information that is not approved for publication.

Keep original files outside the repository. Use descriptive filenames such as:

```text
MOBILE-IOS-001-title-screenshot-redacted.png
```
